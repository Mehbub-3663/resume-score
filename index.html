ATS RESUME GUIDE

What is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System, commonly called an ATS, is software employers may use to organize and screen job applications. A resume with clear structure, readable formatting, relevant skills, and specific achievements can be easier for both software and recruiters to understand.

ResumeScore helps you identify common resume issues before you apply. Your score is an informational ATS-readiness estimate, not a guarantee that a specific employer's ATS will accept or reject your resume.
